processes a zero-height command-01 request, permits a synchronous callback to
rewrite the next link, reloads that link, and finishes the windowed surface
tail. Its `70C0(0xFF)` remap request skips the pixel loop because of the seeded
guard state. Controlled StretchDIBits/GdiFlush endpoints verify arguments and
order; neither original Windows drawing nor pixel equality is claimed.

An interception of slot89 must preserve these state changes, live-link reloads,
notifications and surface/present effects. A single entry snapshot cannot
replace the subsequently consumed mutable stream. Other texture, movie,
palette, desktop-DC text and mode writers remain independent routes. Their
order against a real gameplay frame is still open. Primitive family alone
does not identify a persistent world or UI layer.

The smallest supported deployment candidate remains an app-local DDRAW loader
with a stable selective callback-table clone and transparent forwarding.
Loader selection and renderer interception are separate boundaries. The
source-built controller adds tested observation machinery; it does not grant
display ownership or prove a safe original-game launch. Verified writable-path
and registry/profile isolation, windowed startup, module identity checks and
display restoration remain prerequisites for that experiment.

No GPU backend, SDL integration, gameplay rewrite, original-process launch or
