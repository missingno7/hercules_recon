# AC60 consumer-to-contract coverage

Prediction: every AC60 route represented in the public contract either decodes its accessed bytes and exposes state-callee inputs, or remains explicitly unknown. A falsifier is an unexported consumed input, an incorrect consumer minimum, or a fallthrough side effect not surfaced to the adapter.

This integrated coverage audit reuses published packet, closure, module-producer, native-staging, and submission-gate receipts. It incorporates the bounded group-0x60 correction and keeps producer uncertainty explicit. Oracle: `HERCULES.EXE` SHA-256 `587ae2e90fd2d1827dab6a2745340240e500846f974f718278e2a50199769c1d`; AC60 RVA `0xAC60`, size `0x690`, SHA-256 `f7724c04bef582e378b183a12550ba841df1a6fa3660be51da71154aa5c525af`.

## Integrated staging correction

The public packet decoder remains unchanged and keeps unknown packet kinds unknown. The staging helper publishes `UNRECOGNIZED_GROUP60` for a complete, unrecognized 8-byte group-`0x60` header, returns `APPLY_UNRECOGNIZED`, and conditionally refreshes the page base before publishing the event. It increments the adapter's dispatch sequence consistently with its other published events. Other unknown tags remain unchanged, and known sprites continue through their prior branch.

The 51-assertion C89 fixture checks tags `0x60`, `0x62`, and `0x70` under gate values 0, 1, and `0x100`; it asserts non-success decode status, unknown packet kind, event publication, page state, and sequence. It also checks exact `0x68`, recognized `0x64`, non-group unknown `0x55`, and a short header. GCC strict C89 and pinned VC5 C89 builds both pass. The trace receipt proves the adjacent-byte-only case in original x86 code, since the C state view correctly stores the gate as one byte.

## Findings and limits

No packet/state input is missing on producer-backed FT4, GT4, variable-sprite, E1, F3, or command-01 routes. Direct-read minima remain separate from producer strides and command-01's transitive pointee extent. FT4/GT4 fields were not re-derived.

The confirmed complete-output boundary remains command `0x01`: its inputs decode fully, but the adapter defers A8F0's compatibility copy and completion notification. Group `0x40` naming remains an optional decoder convenience. Unknown group-`0x60` tags have the newly confirmed conditional page-base effect; the private event preserves it without claiming a draw success or a packet layout. Their first-party producer status remains `UNRESOLVED`.

Proof labels are separate: original instruction behavior is `STATIC_OBSERVED`; packed declarations are `LAYOUT_VERIFIED`; existing finite native receipts and the new 12-vector trace are `BOUNDED_EQUIVALENT` only for their recorded boundaries; unobserved producer extents/occurrences stay `UNRESOLVED`.
