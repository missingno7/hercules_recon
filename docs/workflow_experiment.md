# Worker policy and actor continuation

The project keeps its existing evidence → scratch → measurement → promotion path.
`recovery.json` is still the sole accepted state. The later user refinement replaces
the initial tiered model proposal: **Luna xhigh normally; Sol high when uncertainty
itself is the problem.** There is no routing ledger or automatic selector.

## What transferred from Blood II

The reference was inspected at commit
[`53e128f5a8a3e441b51bd7cd06dd6d59f0e2065d`](https://github.com/missingno7/blood2_recon/blob/53e128f5a8a3e441b51bd7cd06dd6d59f0e2065d/docs/WORKFLOW.md),
including its workflow, agent contract, consolidation report and context/knowledge
tools. No implementation was copied. Useful principles are an interpretable region
boundary, progressive context, private experiments, immutable compact results,
outcome deduplication, explicit negative evidence, and one integrator.

Hercules does not inherit Blood II's mature TU/source ownership, per-image state
format, multi-toolchain orchestration or whole-image gate. Our contiguous region
is explicitly inferred. Existing accepted state and fresh VC5 raw contribution
checks remain unchanged. We did not add a database, distributed scheduler, compiler
cache, automated garbage collector, automatic difficulty scores or model-review gate.

The minimum additions are a context/receipt utility and a reproducible measurement
adapter for this region's real inline switch tables. The old linear disassembler
cannot safely treat those tables as instructions. Complete raw comparisons retain
all 56 table bytes; indirect-switch CFG similarity is not claimed.

## Research priors and actual routing

[RetroBench v0.4.8](https://retrobench.ai/) reports five N64/MIPS/IDO targets and
compiler-enforced build budgets. Its displayed means favor Sol (94.9) over Luna
(60.4), but these are neither Hercules results nor a controlled test of our two
chosen effort settings. A detailed 99.6 result appears rounded to 100 in the top
table; strict equality must remain a separate Boolean. Useful lessons are explicit
compiler conditions, build counts and resumable runs—not adopting a universal
25-build quota. No benchmark run was reproduced here.

The [Artificial Analysis comparison](https://artificialanalysis.ai/models/releases/comparisons/gpt-6-1-sol-vs-gpt-6-luna)
is a broader prior. Neither site's aggregate scores supply actual Hercules cost
or justify escalation from similarity alone.

Actual assignments were Sol high for the initial uncertain shared region and
Luna high for mechanical historical-outcome auditing, both dispatched under the
earlier policy. After the user refinement, Luna xhigh took diagnosed local
reconstruction and an independent natural-link dependency investigation. Sol was
not used as their routine reviewer. Requested configurations are known; runtime
identity, tokens and billed cost were not exposed and are recorded as null.

## Granularity and worker experience

Seven previously uncompiled entries cover ENG1 `0x219e0..0x21f5f` (1,408 bytes).
Timers, spawn callbacks, particle motion and owner-derived scale share a measured
actor layout and calls. This is a coherent feedback boundary, not a quota or a
claimed historical TU. The lead reconstructed all seven before tuning.

The first build produced one raw exact contribution (48 bytes), three
relocation-only diagnostic equals (464 bytes), and three residuals. The common
layout held; local store ordering, call-tail merging and index lifetime explained
the residuals, so the work shrank immediately to local families. The original
four equal contributions were frozen. The full first-pass harness passed 479,026
checks. Finite synthetic tests support semantics; they do not prove all behavior.

`scripts/region.py packet actor_continuation` now gives a lead source/context
identity, current recovery overlay, retained frontier, failed families and next
discriminator. A worker receives a private lane and a concrete hypothesis, measures
ordinary C with pinned VC5, returns source patches plus measurements, and leaves
publication to the integrator. The first receipt was ingested before final local
consolidation; the worker exercised the packet and preflight.

The initial lead waited while the context helper was being built, exposing real
coordination overhead. Its duration was not separately measured. The revised
policy removed that dependency: a missing packet must never hold up an already
bounded experiment. This run is evidence that the workflow functions, not a
controlled demonstration that the added process saves total time.

## Candidate and knowledge lifecycle

Source patches are bound to the baseline hash. Different spellings with identical
full contribution bytes and relocation tuples share an outcome identity. Object
timestamps and paths do not influence it. Selected immutable receipts and failed
family summaries are WARM evidence; generated packets, objects, executables and
verbose reports remain ignored. This is diagnostic context, never cached proof.
Regressing raw-exact peers are excluded; recovery closure removes closed work from
the packet. Old source/target/build context is identified as stale on resume.

The historical audit found 60 available measured objects from 68 recorded refinery
attempts, collapsing to 19 distinct selected-function outcomes: 41 duplicates.
Eight missing metrics files are listed explicitly. This demonstrates repeated
outcomes, not 41 builds that could certainly have been avoided beforehand. The
audit ran no fresh compiler. Exhausted families retain predictions, falsifiers,
identities and re-entry conditions. Legacy unindexed contexts remain advisory.

The new tag-lookup investigation tested a previously untried cached-key source
axis, after rereading the target's cursor initialization around the count branch.
Both forms passed semantics and protected peers but worsened code generation.
They were rejected, and the family stopped. Work moved to the real release/data
dependencies instead of another syntax grid. No automatic retry ladder was used.

## Production result and limits

| Measurement | First pass | Final |
|---|---:|---:|
| Raw-exact functions / bytes | 1 / 48 | 2 / 144 |
| Relocation-only diagnostic equals / bytes | 3 / 464 | 5 / 1,264 |
| Source/codegen residuals | 3 | 0 |

Luna's first owner-coordinate reorder removed nine raw differences. Branch-local
spawn callbacks restored separate call/test tails and diagnostic equality. Moving
the particle counter out of function entry restored the early z register; spelling
the repeated switch arms separately restored the ten-entry table. A final reread
located the ten-byte tail: initialize the counter immediately *before* the discarded
RNG call. That single ordinary reorder closed the final diagnostic difference.
No Sol escalation was needed for these bounded tails.

The two raw functions, `initialize_linked_particle` and `follow_owner_scale`, passed
canonical isolation, 521 preservation/boundary/alias checks and the unchanged normal
promotion transaction. All 38 accepted instances freshly verified. Current totals
are **38 accepted instances, 29 source functions and 4,320 mapped bytes** (+144).
Five relocated contributions remain experimental. No whole DLL equality, natural
layout, original TU identity or uniquely identified historical source is claimed.

Discovery used one Sol region object plus one semantic build, followed by seven
Luna objects plus seven semantic builds: **16 compiler invocations, no failures**.
Two Luna objects were combined-contribution checks, not new hypotheses. All eight
full-region semantic runs passed 479,026 checks each. Reported object compile/compare
time totals 7.653 seconds; semantic compilation/model/coordination times were not
separately available. Core research, implementation and consolidation took about
36 minutes wall time, including coordination and review—not a model latency score.
Tokens and actual costs remain unknown, so no comparative cost-efficiency claim is
made from this single run.

Integration additionally used four replay objects (including a restored-recipe
compile), two replay semantic attempts, one canonical-isolation object/harness,
38 promotion compilations and nine registered semantic suites. One replay harness
attempt failed because the new CLI passed a relative output directory; resolving
that directory fixed it. This process error is counted separately from successful
worker reconstruction builds. The restored receipt independently reproduces 2 raw
and 7 diagnostic equals. All nine suites passed **600,020 checks**; **26 Python
tests** and **12 immutable original hashes** passed. Original macro/refinery sources
and prior accepted source/proof rows remain unchanged.

`evidence/actor_continuation_run.json` records yield, builds, actual requested
configurations, targeted predictions and limitations. `evidence/regions/actor_final.json`
retains the final source recipe. Full compiler outputs remain ignored.

## Current bottleneck

This region's source forms are now understood; rewriting its five relocated equals
has no demonstrated value. Natural linkage still needs real code/data contributions,
their dependencies and historically justified grouping. The tag lookup remains a
source-order residual in the earlier link interval. Its cached-key family added
five compiler invocations (three objects, two semantic harnesses) and no retained
improvement. The next concrete layer is the release routines plus real scene-anchor
and tag-list data extents. The dependency follow-up records those boundaries and
keeps unresolved hypotheses separate from accepted code.

That follow-up reached the actual release layer. `release_actor` at `0x27e20`
has a reviewed 304-byte contribution; its first ordinary-C hypothesis emits 288
bytes, retains 181 normalized differences and changes CFG, despite matching all
ten direct-call destinations. Twelve synthetic branch checks pass, which is only
a small probe. The source remains in calibration, with no recovery promotion.
One worker object and one harness were compiled, then independently reproduced
after preservation. `packet release_actor` provides the compact continuation.

The 137-byte `release_buffer` at `0x7cde` accesses a 24-entry resource table with
64-byte stride. Its helpers dispatch through virtual-data cells `0x71098`,
`0x710a0`, and `0x712a4`, beyond file-backed raw data. Their initializer, owner and
calling ABI are unknown. Actor/resource-specific accesses provisionally suggest
engine code; no standard-library provenance has been identified. Substituting
CRT/Win32 freeing routines would be unsupported. This is a concrete high-leverage
initialization/ownership question for future Sol high diagnosis if the existing
cross-reference evidence does not clarify it.

The additional preserved release probe brings registered semantic validation to
**ten suites / 600,032 checks**, with zero failures. Release evidence is in
`evidence/release_dependency.json`; the earlier tag experiments remain separate
in `evidence/linkage_dependency_followup.json`.

Reusable lesson: broad context helped establish representation, then ordinary local
diagnoses gave Luna useful work. The evidence supports that coarse-to-fine sequence
here; it does not establish a universal best region size or prove one model tier's
cost superiority. Process overhead should remain subordinate to actual recovery.
